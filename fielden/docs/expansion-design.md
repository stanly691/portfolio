# FIELDEN identity and shopping journey

FIELDEN stands for finding your own way: everyday streets, outdoor trails, and the time in between. The identity pairs an original vector F trail-cut symbol and geometric wordmark with Barlow Condensed headlines and Space Grotesk interface text. The wordmark is custom lettering; the website fonts are licensed open-source families, not a newly invented font.

Palette: charcoal #151a15, chalk #f5f5ef, orange #f45b35. Rectangular controls, compact product labels, generous image areas, and large uppercase campaign headlines form the shared system. Vivobarefoot informed the outdoor commerce hierarchy; FIELDEN uses original branding, copy and generated imagery.

Routes: home → collection with activity/material filters and price sorting → full product page with gallery, zoom, size selection and care details → bag with editable quantities → selection review. Hash URLs support direct links and browser history on GitHub Pages. Search, saved items, brand story, journal and help have full pages. No product popups.

24 generated image compositions, including 12 product concepts and alternate views. Two six-second editorial camera-motion loops are supplied as GIF and smaller animated WebP; these animate still compositions, not simulated live action. Visibility and reduced-motion preferences control playback; each loop can be paused.

This is a frontend design concept. No orders, payments, accounts or newsletter submissions are connected. Bag and saved state exist in page memory.
