# FIELDEN — lifestyle ecommerce design concept

Separate project requested by the user, with creative direction and naming delegated. Reference: https://www.vivobarefoot.com/uk/ (reviewed 19 November 2026). Use the reference's campaign-first scale, category discovery and considered product presentation. Develop an original, more classic editorial identity rather than its seasonal sale campaign.

Direction: FIELDEN, a fictional footwear brand. Quiet outdoor luxury. Ivory #f7f5ef, olive #343b2d, charcoal #252922, stone #e8e5de. Cormorant Garamond display typography and DM Sans utility typography. Wide margins, square corners, hairline dividers, minimal motion and real photographic scale.

Page: slim seasonal announcement; understated navigation; coastal image hero; concise brand introduction; four-product collection with filter; paired women's/men's campaign tiles; materials editorial; field notes; olive newsletter/footer with oversized wordmark. Responsive React + TypeScript, self-hosted fonts, locally saved generated WebP imagery. Native dialogs for product detail, bag, search and editorial content. Local demo interactions only; no checkout, account, newsletter transmission or backend.

Acceptance: no horizontal overflow at 320–1920px; all assets local and loaded; keyboard-operable navigation/dialogs, labelled controls, reduced motion; build/typecheck passes. Preserve existing ITCS app. Website imagery generated with the built-in image generation tool; brand/products/prices are illustrative.
